FAQ

Common Questions

01 How long does a project take? +

Fastest delivery is 48 hours. Larger projects like full brand identities typically take 5–10 days. We'll always provide a clear timeline before we start.

02 What file formats do you deliver? +

We deliver all source files (AI, PSD, Figma) plus export-ready versions (PNG, SVG, PDF, MP4). You always own your files completely.

03 Do you offer package deals? +

Yes! We offer combined packages — logo + sticker + business card, menu + social media, and more. New clients also receive 10% OFF their first order.

04 Do you work with international clients? +

Absolutely. While we're Cairo-based, we serve clients across the Arab world and internationally. Communication is seamless via WhatsApp, email, and Zoom.
